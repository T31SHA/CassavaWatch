How alerts work

Each 0.1° grid cell's cases from the last 7 days are compared with what its own 60-day baseline predicts for the number of reports it received.

An alert needs at least 5 cases, twice the expected count and a Poisson p-value below 0.01, so busy areas with a normal disease mix do not trigger alerts.
