ⓘ How alerts work

Each 0.1° cell's last-7-day positives are compared with what its own 60-day baseline rate predicts for the number of reports it received this week; an alert needs ≥5 cases, ≥2× expected and Poisson p<0.01. Because the expected count scales with report volume, a place with many active reporters but a normal disease mix does not alert — unlike raw density hotspots.
